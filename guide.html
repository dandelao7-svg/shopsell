01 Get started

Create your store Live

Signing up takes a couple of minutes and starts a free trial — no credit card needed.

  1. Go to /signup and enter your name, email, and a password. The password box shows five requirements (8+ characters, upper, lower, number, symbol) that turn green as you type — the eye icon reveals what you typed.
  2. Pick your store name and a store URL slug (this becomes part of your web address — lowercase letters, numbers, and dashes only). It's suggested for you from the store name; you can edit it.
  3. Choose a plan tile (Starter / Growth / Pro) and click Create my store. Your 14-day free trial begins immediately.

Logging back in

Use /login. Forgot your password? Click Forgot password — a reset link is emailed to you (this now works; it used to fail silently). The link is good for one hour.

03 Build your store

Products, photos & storefront pages Live

Add a product

Open Add product and fill in the essentials — name, description, category, SKU (there's an Auto-generate button), price, and quantity. As you set price and cost, ShopSell shows your margin live.

  • Options & variants — add up to three options (e.g. Size, Color) and ShopSell builds a row for every combination, each with its own price, SKU, and stock.
  • Images — drag and drop up to six photos (or click to browse). The first one is your main photo. They're shrunk automatically so your store stays fast.
  • Returns policy — mark an item returnable, set a return window, and an optional restocking fee.
  • Customs (international shipping) — HS code, country of origin, and a plain customs description. Fill these in only if you ship abroad; they auto-fill the customs form on international labels. There's also a Not for international shipping checkbox that blocks the item from cross-border orders. See section 8.
  • Click Publish product to make it live.

Heads-up: use “Publish”, not “Save draft”

The Save draft button doesn't store anything yet — it just shows a message. To keep a product, use Publish product (you can set its status to Draft in the form if you don't want it public).

Import a whole catalog from a spreadsheet

Moving from another store? On the Products page click Import products to bring in many products at once from a CSV (spreadsheet) file.

  • Any CSV works — you don't have to rename your columns. After you upload, a Match your columns step lets you tell ShopSell which of your columns is the name, price, SKU, and so on. It auto-matches most columns for you (it recognises common names like Title, Selling Price, Qty, Product Type, Image URL); just fix any that look wrong. If you'd rather start clean, Download CSV template gives you the exact columns.
  • Only name, category, price, sku are required, and each SKU must be unique. No category column? Pick a Default category in the matching step and it's applied to every row.
  • Check the preview, then click Import products. You'll get a summary showing how many were created, skipped, or had an error — with the reason for each. Re-importing the same file is safe: products whose SKU already exists are skipped, not duplicated.
  • Variants aren't supported here yet — each row becomes one simple product. Add options/variants afterward on the Edit product screen.

About photos in an import

Photos are added as web links, not uploaded files. In the imageUrls column paste a public image address (e.g. https://…/photo.jpg) — easy if your products are already on Shopify, eBay, Etsy or a website (right-click a photo → “Copy image address”). Put several in one cell separated by a space or |, or leave it blank and add photos later. To upload photo files straight from your computer, use the normal Add product screen instead.

Storefront pages & discounts

Under Storefront, fill in your contact email and your About / FAQ / Privacy / Terms text — these appear in your store's footer. Under Discounts, create a code (percentage or fixed amount), optionally with a minimum order, usage limit, or expiry date.

Make it yours — theme & branding Live

Still under Storefront, the Theme & branding card restyles your whole store — no code:

  • Theme preset — pick Department, Boutique, or Minimal. Each re-skins the entire store (colors, corner style, shadows, fonts).
  • Accent color — set your brand color (or “Use theme default”). It drives buttons, prices, and highlights.
  • Logo — upload an image (shown in the header) or leave it blank to show your store name.

Homepage & banner Live

The Homepage & banner card controls the top of your store:

  • Announcement bar — the thin banner across the very top (e.g. “Free shipping on every order”), with an optional link. Toggle on/off.
  • Hero — set the big eyebrow, headline, subtext, and both button labels, and upload a hero background image (a dark overlay is added automatically so the text stays readable). Any field left blank keeps the default.

Page layout — rearrange your homepage Live

The Page layout card (top of Storefront) treats your homepage as a stack of sections you can reorder, hide, edit, add, or remove — no code. Today's building blocks:

SectionWhat it isWhat you can edit
HeroThe big image + headline at the top.Eyebrow, headline, subtext, background image URL.
Featured carouselThe auto-scrolling row of starred products.Heading.
Product gridYour main shop grid.Show/hide the category tabs.
Promo bannerA deal bar with an optional live countdown — good for a sale ending Friday.Text, countdown date, solid or outline style.

Each section in the list has ↑ / ↓ to move it, a toggle to enable or disable it, and a delete button. Pick a type from the dropdown and click + Add section to add another (you can have more than one promo banner, for example).

Draft first, then publish

Save draft stores your changes privately — shoppers keep seeing the old layout. Preview ↗ opens your store showing the draft (with a banner so you know it's a preview); nobody else sees it. When you're happy, click Publish to make it live. Discard draft throws the draft away; Reset to default returns you to the standard hero + carousel + grid layout.

Nothing changes until you touch it

If you never open Page layout, your store renders exactly as it always has. The Theme, Homepage & banner, and Featured-carousel cards below still work the same way — Page layout is the extra control on top.

Featured carousel Live

On the Products page, flip on Featured carousel and click the ★ on any products. They appear in an auto-scrolling row at the top of your homepage. Turn the toggle off to hide the whole row without un-starring anything.

Email capture — grow a marketing list Live

The Email capture card (under Storefront) turns on a popup that offers visitors a discount for their email:

  • Set the discount % and optional headline/subtext, then toggle it on. Visitors see the popup once.
  • When someone signs up, ShopSell creates a unique one-time discount code, emails it to them, and adds them to your Subscribers list — which you can Export to CSV for future marketing.
  • The code works at checkout like any other discount.

05 Get paid

Connect your Stripe account Live

ShopSell doesn't hold your money — customer payments go straight into your own Stripe account. You connect it once.

  1. In Stripe, open Developers → API keys and copy your Secret key and Publishable key.
  2. In ShopSell, open Stripe setup, paste both keys, and click Save keys. Use Test connection to confirm they work.
  3. Copy the Webhook endpoint URL ShopSell shows you into Stripe (Developers → Webhooks), then paste the signing secret Stripe gives back into ShopSell.

Test mode vs live mode

Stripe gives you test keys and live keys. Test keys let you place practice orders with Stripe's test cards (like 4242 4242 4242 4242) — no real money moves. Switch to your live keys when you're ready for real customers.

Until you connect Stripe, your storefront checkout shows “Payment is not configured for this store.”

What your customers can pay with New · not live-tested

Checkout now uses Stripe's modern Payment Element — a single payment box that automatically offers everything your Stripe account has enabled:

  • Cards — as before.
  • Stripe Link — returning shoppers who've used Link anywhere get their saved card filled in from an email code. That's where “saved cards” come from; ShopSell never stores card details itself.
  • Apple Pay / Google Pay — shown automatically on supported devices.
  • Anything else you turn on in Stripe (e.g. Klarna, Cash App Pay) appears here without any change in ShopSell. Manage the list in Stripe → Settings → Payment methods.

Two things before you take real money

1. Place a test order first. This checkout was rebuilt recently and hasn't been run against a real Stripe account yet. Connect test keys, buy something on your own storefront, and confirm the payment box loads and the order lands in Orders.

2. Apple Pay needs domain verification. In Stripe → Settings → Payment methods → Apple Pay, add your store's domain. Without it, Apple Pay simply won't appear.

07 Ship your orders

The shipping suite

Open Shipping from the dashboard. This is where you connect carriers, compare rates, buy labels, and track packages.

Connect your carriers Live

Under Settings in the shipping screen, add your own UPS, FedEx, and/or USPS account credentials and click Save & verify. As with Stripe, you bring your own carrier accounts. Each connected carrier now has a Disconnect button if you need to clear out bad credentials and start over.

UPS: use a production app

If UPS rejects everything with error 250002, your UPS developer app isn't provisioned for production. Create/promote the app in the UPS developer portal, then paste the updated Client ID + Secret into ShopSell Settings — that's the only place that matters. Carrier errors now show you the carrier's own error code, which makes this kind of thing much faster to spot.

Buy a label — use Quick Ship Live

Quick Ship is the real, end-to-end label flow. This is the one to use.

  1. Pick who it ships from, then enter the recipient (typing a ZIP auto-fills city/state).
  2. Choose a box size (or enter dimensions) and the weight.
  3. Click Compare rates to price-shop your connected carriers live — cheapest first — and pick one.
  4. Click Generate shipping label. ShopSell validates the address, buys a real label with real tracking, saves it to History, and prints it.

Buy labels in Quick Ship

On the Orders list, the per-order Create + Print Label and Get rate buttons, and the top-bar Print, now send you to Quick Ship. (They used to fake a tracking number without actually buying anything — that's fixed, but the real purchase happens in Quick Ship.)

Other shipping tools

  • Batch labels Live — tick several orders, choose “cheapest” or a fixed service, and buy them all at once, then print one merged PDF.
  • Packing slips & pick lists Live — generate real PDFs for one order or a whole batch.
  • Tracking Live — paste a tracking number to see live status and history.
  • History Live — every label you've bought: reprint, void, or export to CSV.
  • Pickup & Automation Live — schedule carrier pickups and set rules (e.g. “orders under 1 lb → USPS”).
  • Thermal printer Live — a wizard sets up a Zebra 4×6 label printer.

08 Ship internationally

Selling and shipping across borders Live

ShopSell used to be US-only. It now handles overseas orders end to end — foreign addresses at checkout, international rates and services, and the customs paperwork filled in for you.

The short version

A buyer abroad checks out → the order captures their foreign address → you buy a label from Orders or Quick Ship → ShopSell builds the customs declaration from your product data and sends it to the carrier. In the common case there is nothing extra for you to fill in.

1 · Tell ShopSell about your products

On Add product (and Edit product) there's a Customs (international shipping) box:

  • HS code — the Harmonized System number that classifies the item for customs. Your supplier or invoice usually has it; otherwise search the official HS lookup for your category.
  • Country of origin — the 2-letter code for where the item was made (e.g. CN), not where you ship from.
  • Customs description — a plain description customs will understand (“cotton t-shirt”, not “Midnight Fade Tee”). Defaults to the product name if you leave it blank.
  • Not for international shipping — tick this for anything you can't or won't send abroad (batteries, aerosols, liquids, restricted goods). Overseas buyers are blocked from ordering it at checkout, and international labels for orders containing it are refused.

You can skip this at first

All of it is optional — leave it blank for domestic-only items. If a product has no customs data, ShopSell falls back to the product name and value, which is enough for most low-value shipments.

2 · Choose who pays duties & taxes

Under Storefront → International shipping, set Duties & taxes paid by:

  • Recipient — collected on delivery (DDU) — the default. The carrier collects import charges from your customer before handing the parcel over.
  • Me / the store — prepaid (DDP) — you're billed for duties on your carrier account. Smoother for the buyer, more expensive for you.

Overseas shoppers see a matching notice at checkout (“import duties & taxes may be collected by the carrier on delivery,” or “are prepaid, nothing extra to pay”), so there are no surprises at the door. The setting also flows onto the customs paperwork. You can override it per shipment in Quick Ship.

3 · Buy the international label

In Quick Ship, pick a country other than the United States and the form adapts:

  • The State dropdown becomes a free-text region/province box (US, Canada, UK, and Australia keep their dropdowns), and postal-code rules relax.
  • The service list gains UPS Worldwide, FedEx International, and USPS International options, and Compare rates price-shops the real cross-border routes.
  • A 🌍 Customs declaration panel appears. Set the contents type (merchandise, gift, sample, documents, returned goods) and incoterm (DDU/DDP), then add a line for each item: description, quantity, value, HS code, and origin. + Add item adds more rows. You need at least one item with a description and a value above zero before ShopSell will generate the label.

Buying a label for a real order is easier — ShopSell assembles the customs declaration automatically from the order's line items and your product customs fields. The manual panel exists because Quick Ship shipments have no order behind them.

4 · Paperwork

  • Commercial invoice — when the carrier returns a printable customs invoice (UPS accounts not enrolled in Paperless Invoice), a Print customs invoice button appears next to the label. Print three copies and attach them to the parcel in a documents pouch.
  • UPS Paperless Invoice Needs UPS setup — enroll your UPS account once with UPS and the invoice is transmitted electronically instead, so there's nothing to print. Until you enroll, use the printed copies above.
  • Export filing (AES/EEL) — shipments under $2,500 per commodity are filed automatically with the standard low-value exemption; you do nothing. At $2,500 and above US law requires an AES filing: file with the government, then paste the ITN you get back into the AES ITN field in Quick Ship (format AES X20180101987654).

What your overseas customers see

Checkout offers a full country list (67 countries), with a region dropdown for the US, Canada, UK, and Australia and a free-text region everywhere else. They get the duties notice described above, and any product you flagged Not for international shipping stops the order with a clear explanation rather than failing later.

Two honest caveats

Not yet proven on a live cross-border label. The customs code is built to each carrier's published specification and unit-tested, but no international label has been purchased on a real account here yet. Your first overseas shipment is the real test — do a cheap one.

No denied-party screening and no landed-cost quote. ShopSell doesn't check recipients against restricted-party lists, and buyers see a duties notice, not a calculated duty amount. Both need a paid third-party data source; they're on the list, not built.

09 Returns

Let customers print a return label Live

Your customers handle returns themselves through the return portal — you don't have to do anything per return, beyond setting your return address.

  1. Set your return address once (dashboard → Settings) and make sure a carrier is connected.
  2. A customer goes to /returns, enters their order number and email, and picks the items to send back.
  3. ShopSell generates a prepaid return label on your carrier account and emails it to them.

Two different “Returns” screens

The customer-facing portal at /returns is the real one. The Returns tab inside the Shipping suite currently shows sample data only — don't manage real returns from there yet.

10 What your customers see

The shopping experience

Here's the buyer's side, so you know what you're offering.

  • Browse — a storefront with your products, categories, sorting, and search; each product opens a detail view with photos, variants, quantity, and reviews.
  • Cart & checkout — add to cart, then check out with contact info and a shipping address. Checkout requires a name, a valid email, and a full address before charging, and accepts addresses worldwide — 67 countries, with region dropdowns for the US, Canada, UK, and Australia and free-text regions elsewhere.
  • Paying — one payment box offering cards, Stripe Link (saved cards), and Apple Pay / Google Pay on supported devices, plus anything else you've enabled in Stripe. See section 5.
  • Overseas orders — buyers outside the US see a duties & taxes notice matching your DDU/DDP setting, and can't order items you flagged as not shippable internationally.
  • Accounts — customers can create an account to see their order history; past guest orders link automatically by email.

Good to know

Payment on your storefront runs on your Stripe account. Reviews, order confirmation emails, and shipping-notification emails are all wired up and working.

11 Plans & billing

Your ShopSell subscription Partial

You picked a plan at signup (Starter / Growth / Pro) and you're on a 14-day free trial. Your plan and history live under My subscription, where Change plan and Cancel subscription are wired to real Stripe subscriptions.

During beta

Subscription billing still runs against ShopSell's Stripe test account, so no one is charged real money yet. Manage billing in Stripe → needs Stripe's Customer Portal switched on for the platform account; until that's done it returns an error rather than opening.

13 Tips & troubleshooting

Handy to know

  • Store looks empty? The storefront needs a store context. View store handles this for you; a bare link without ?store=your-slug will look blank.
  • Checkout says payment isn't configured? Connect Stripe (section 5).
  • Return portal says returns aren't available? Set your return address in Settings and connect a carrier.
  • Didn't get the password-reset email? Check spam on the first one from a new store, then mark it “not spam.”
  • Layout changes not showing to customers? You probably clicked Save draft. Drafts are private — click Publish.
  • No international services in Compare rates? Set the destination country first — the service list and rates only switch to cross-border routes once the recipient country isn't the US.
  • “Add at least one customs item”? International labels need a customs declaration. Fill in the 🌍 panel with a description and a value above $0 for at least one item.
  • An item won't ship abroad? Check whether it's ticked Not for international shipping on the product.
  • Carrier rejecting everything? The error now includes the carrier's own code — search that code, and use Disconnect in Shipping → Settings to re-enter credentials cleanly.
  • Practice safely. With Stripe in test mode, use test card 4242 4242 4242 4242, any future expiry, any CVC — nothing is charged.
