01 Get started

Create your store Live

Signing up takes a couple of minutes and starts a free trial — no credit card needed.

  1. Go to /signup and enter your name, email, and a password. The password box shows five requirements (8+ characters, upper, lower, number, symbol) that turn green as you type — the eye icon reveals what you typed.
  2. Pick your store name and a store URL slug (this becomes part of your web address — lowercase letters, numbers, and dashes only). It's suggested for you from the store name; you can edit it.
  3. Choose a plan tile (Starter / Growth / Pro) and click Create my store. Your 14-day free trial begins immediately.

Logging back in

Use /login. Forgot your password? Click Forgot password — a reset link is emailed to you (this now works; it used to fail silently). The link is good for one hour.

03 Build your store

Products, photos & storefront pages Live

Add a product

Open Add product and fill in the essentials — name, description, category, SKU (there's an Auto-generate button), price, and quantity. As you set price and cost, ShopSell shows your margin live.

  • Options & variants — add up to three options (e.g. Size, Color) and ShopSell builds a row for every combination, each with its own price, SKU, and stock.
  • Images — drag and drop up to six photos (or click to browse). The first one is your main photo. They're shrunk automatically so your store stays fast.
  • Returns policy — mark an item returnable, set a return window, and an optional restocking fee.
  • Click Publish product to make it live.

Heads-up: use “Publish”, not “Save draft”

The Save draft button doesn't store anything yet — it just shows a message. To keep a product, use Publish product (you can set its status to Draft in the form if you don't want it public).

Storefront pages & discounts

Under Storefront, fill in your contact email and your About / FAQ / Privacy / Terms text — these appear in your store's footer. Under Discounts, create a code (percentage or fixed amount), optionally with a minimum order, usage limit, or expiry date.

05 Get paid

Connect your Stripe account Live

ShopSell doesn't hold your money — customer payments go straight into your own Stripe account. You connect it once.

  1. In Stripe, open Developers → API keys and copy your Secret key and Publishable key.
  2. In ShopSell, open Stripe setup, paste both keys, and click Save keys. Use Test connection to confirm they work.
  3. Copy the Webhook endpoint URL ShopSell shows you into Stripe (Developers → Webhooks), then paste the signing secret Stripe gives back into ShopSell.

Test mode vs live mode

Stripe gives you test keys and live keys. Test keys let you place practice orders with Stripe's test cards (like 4242 4242 4242 4242) — no real money moves. Switch to your live keys when you're ready for real customers.

Until you connect Stripe, your storefront checkout shows “Payment is not configured for this store.”

What “Built · needs setup” means

The code for these channels is done — but, exactly like eBay was, each marketplace needs its own developer-app registration + credentials and a one-time connection test before it works. Turning one on is the same short process we did for eBay. Only eBay is fully live today.

You'll find these in the Shipping screen under “Sales channels.” Once connected, click Sync on a channel to pull in its unshipped orders. Re-syncing is safe — orders won't duplicate. When you buy a shipping label for one of those orders, ShopSell marks it shipped back on the marketplace automatically.

07 Ship your orders

The shipping suite

Open Shipping from the dashboard. This is where you connect carriers, compare rates, buy labels, and track packages.

Connect your carriers Live

Under Settings in the shipping screen, add your own UPS, FedEx, and/or USPS account credentials and click Save & verify. As with Stripe, you bring your own carrier accounts.

Buy a label — use Quick Ship Live

Quick Ship is the real, end-to-end label flow. This is the one to use.

  1. Pick who it ships from, then enter the recipient (typing a ZIP auto-fills city/state).
  2. Choose a box size (or enter dimensions) and the weight.
  3. Click Compare rates to price-shop your connected carriers live — cheapest first — and pick one.
  4. Click Generate shipping label. ShopSell validates the address, buys a real label with real tracking, saves it to History, and prints it.

Buy labels in Quick Ship

On the Orders list, the per-order Create + Print Label and Get rate buttons, and the top-bar Print, now send you to Quick Ship. (They used to fake a tracking number without actually buying anything — that's fixed, but the real purchase happens in Quick Ship.)

Other shipping tools

  • Batch labels Live — tick several orders, choose “cheapest” or a fixed service, and buy them all at once, then print one merged PDF.
  • Packing slips & pick lists Live — generate real PDFs for one order or a whole batch.
  • Tracking Live — paste a tracking number to see live status and history.
  • History Live — every label you've bought: reprint, void, or export to CSV.
  • Pickup & Automation Live — schedule carrier pickups and set rules (e.g. “orders under 1 lb → USPS”).
  • Thermal printer Live — a wizard sets up a Zebra 4×6 label printer.

08 Returns

Let customers print a return label Live

Your customers handle returns themselves through the return portal — you don't have to do anything per return, beyond setting your return address.

  1. Set your return address once (dashboard → Settings) and make sure a carrier is connected.
  2. A customer goes to /returns, enters their order number and email, and picks the items to send back.
  3. ShopSell generates a prepaid return label on your carrier account and emails it to them.

Two different “Returns” screens

The customer-facing portal at /returns is the real one. The Returns tab inside the Shipping suite currently shows sample data only — don't manage real returns from there yet.

09 What your customers see

The shopping experience

Here's the buyer's side, so you know what you're offering.

  • Browse — a storefront with your products, categories, sorting, and search; each product opens a detail view with photos, variants, quantity, and reviews.
  • Cart & checkout — add to cart, then check out with contact info, shipping address, and a card. Checkout now offers all US states + Canadian/UK/Australian regions and requires name, a valid email, and a full address before charging (recent fixes — it used to allow blank/wrong addresses).
  • Accounts — customers can create an account to see their order history; past guest orders link automatically by email.

Good to know

Payment on your storefront runs on your Stripe account. Reviews, order confirmation emails, and shipping-notification emails are all wired up and working.

12 Tips & troubleshooting

Handy to know

  • Store looks empty? The storefront needs a store context. View store handles this for you; a bare link without ?store=your-slug will look blank.
  • Checkout says payment isn't configured? Connect Stripe (section 5).
  • Return portal says returns aren't available? Set your return address in Settings and connect a carrier.
  • Didn't get the password-reset email? Check spam on the first one from a new store, then mark it “not spam.”
  • Practice safely. With Stripe in test mode, use test card 4242 4242 4242 4242, any future expiry, any CVC — nothing is charged.
