How it works for buyers
1
Buyer requests return
Buyer opens your returns portal, enters their order # and email, then picks the items and reason.
2
Prepaid label issued instantly
A prepaid label is generated on your connected carrier account and emailed to the buyer — no approval step. Requires a return address + carrier in Settings.
3
Buyer ships it back
The package travels to your return address. Every request shows up in the table below with its tracking number.
4
You inspect & refund
When the item arrives, mark the return received here and issue the refund from the order in your dashboard.
